APX Lift Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Draft: have this checked by your solicitor before publishing.

This policy explains what information the APX Lift Planner app (Windows, Mac and Android) collects, why, and your rights. The app is provided by APX Lifting Solutions Ltd ("APX", "we"), www.apxlifting.co.uk, info@apxlifting.co.uk. APX is the data controller.

What stays on your device

Your lift plans, drawings, load charts, photos, reports and branding are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

What the app sends to us

  • Licence details: the licence holder's name, company and email address and the licence ID, which are part of the licence key we issued to you.
  • Device code: a code made from your device's identifier, used to tie a licence to a device and to tell devices apart.
  • Usage time: for each day, the minutes the app was open, the minutes it was in use, and how many times it was started, with the app version and operating system.

We use this to manage licences (including disabling a licence that has not been paid for or is used in breach of the licence agreement), to support you, and to improve the app. Our lawful bases are performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interest in managing and improving our software.

Licence checks

At least once every 30 days the app downloads a list of disabled licence numbers from GitHub (github.com). This sends no personal information, but GitHub receives your device's IP address as with any web request.

Map data

If you use "Get map data", the postcode or address you type is sent to postcodes.io, OpenStreetMap Nominatim and the Overpass API to find the site and its surroundings. These are public services with their own privacy policies. We do not store what you type.

Who processes the data

Usage time is stored in a Google Sheet held in APX's Google account (Google is our processor). We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Licence details are kept for as long as you hold a licence and for six years after, for accounting. Usage records are kept for up to three years.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@apxlifting.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Everything the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Licence keys and the disabled-licence list are digitally signed so they cannot be forged.

Children

The app is for professional use and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update this page if what the app collects changes.
